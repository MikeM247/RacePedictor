## ORBIT character refinement

The Product Owner preferred AI Companion's overall interface and refined the character direction to a mature male coach: athletic, futuristic, older-looking, and more sophisticated, with a slightly more humanoid appearance. The revised [character sheet](02-orbit-character.png) softens the mechanical face into natural contours, a skin-like synthetic surface, blue eyes with visible irises, subtle age lines, and a composed expression. The defined jaw, brushed-silver temple framing, cyan accents, lean runner silhouette, and articulated performance suit preserve his established identity.

The [updated UI board](02-ai-companion.png) uses him consistently in the header and small chat avatars while preserving the preferred general look and feel. The [original character board](02-ai-companion-original.png), [previous athletic refinement](02-ai-companion-athletic-v1.png), and its [character sheet](02-orbit-character-athletic-v1.png) are retained for comparison. The gallery opens on the AI Companion direction by default; the other concepts remain available.

The preceding, more mechanical mature male design is also retained as a [UI board](02-ai-companion-mature-v2.png) and [character sheet](02-orbit-character-mature-v2.png).

## Design review

The three directions were developed and reviewed with a UI/UX design agent. Revisions removed out-of-scope attachment controls, reduced the robot's size during conversation, removed a duplicate opening, clarified message roles, and made mobile training context and race-plan access more prominent.

These are generated concept boards, not pixel specifications. Implement the textual scope and screen-state requirements, including selected record identity, clear context switching, one primary action, accessible contrast and touch targets, and complete essential prescription cautions. Fine typography, spacing, component tokens, and responsive/error-state layouts still need the selected direction's detailed design.

The Product Owner approved AI Companion with the final humanoid male ORBIT character and permitted use of these images on the future site. Dated approved copies are preserved in `approved/` and linked from the architecture handoff. Night Ops and Field Notes remain historical comparison references; they are not additional themes to ship.
